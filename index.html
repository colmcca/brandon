About

I've been playing guitar for over 20 years, but am also proficient in mandolin, ukulele, and bass guitar. I sing and play folk, country, blues, and classic rock tunes with a little bit of Gospel thrown in.

Throughout the years, I've played in multiple bands in different styles ranging from jazz to rock to contemporary Christian music. Whether it is leading worship or playing at local venues, I enjoy using music as an avenue to reach people and bring joy into their lives.

I was fortunate to take guitar lessons with Joel Hoekstra of Whitesnake and Trans Siberian Orchestra for two years and am grateful for all he taught me. I look forward to continue growing as a musician and incorporating different styles into my music within the Cleveland music scene.
